## Hook-on cleat (v18-v20, 2026-11-05)
Why: the v17 print showed the clip could not go on. Its 30 mm dovetail channel had to enter from below the rail, but there are only about 15.5 mm between the rail bottom (Z 25) and the plinth (Z 9.5). Brian approved replacing the rail with a hook-on cleat (2026-11-05).

**Change from the proposal (load direction).** The starting proposal had an upward lip on the sleeve with the clip's hook dropping over it and the weight on the sleeve ledge's top. In that arrangement the clip rests on the sleeve, so when the node hangs, the sleeve pulls down away from the clip and drops off. The part that hangs must have the downward hook. So, as on a French cleat, the sleeve (the hung part) has the downward-facing hook and the clip (fixed to the rim) has the upward lip. Fitting is the same in practice: the node is lowered onto the clip (or the clip is pushed up onto the node) by about 3 mm, high on the back, well clear of the plinth.

Sleeve cleat (sketch SL_cleat on the YZ plane, feature `Cleat`, joined to Sleeve_shell; added material only, no hole in the wall):
- Width X -12 to +12 (24 mm), centred on the back wall (flat face is X +-15.93).
- Profile (Y, Z): underside bevel at 45 deg from the wall (31.7, 55.0) down and out to (34.7, 52.0); outer face Y 34.7 from Z 52.0 to 57.0 (5 mm); top chamfer at 45 deg from (34.7, 57.0) back to the wall at (31.7, 60.0). So it stands 3.0 mm proud, its root on the wall is Z 55 to 60 (5 mm), and it is 3.5 mm thick normal to its 45 deg faces. The hook depth (how far the lip hangs below its root) is 3 mm.
- The top of the cleat is 2.8 mm below the roof (Z 62.8) and its bottom 42.5 mm above the plinth top (Z 9.5).
- The load face is the underside bevel.

Clip receiver (RimClip, replaces the channel; the J, rim gap and pads are unchanged):
- Block 30 mm wide (X +-15), Y 32.05 to 38.2 (the glass-side face Y 38.2 is unchanged), Z 30 to 64. Its top tapers at 45 deg into the 18 mm wide rim hook (from X +-15 at Z 58 to X +-9 at Z 64), and its inner top edge is a 45 deg chamfer from (Y 35.4, Z 64) to (Y 32.05, Z 60.65).
- Pocket (cut X +-12.35, 24.7 mm wide): the clip's lip is a 45 deg bevel from (Y 32.05, Z 54.155) down to (Y 35.05, Z 51.155), parallel to the cleat underside and 0.35 mm from it; pocket back wall Y 35.05 (0.35 mm behind the cleat's outer face); open upward so the cleat drops in.
- Cheeks X +-12.35 to +-15 (2.65 mm thick) reach to Y 32.05, 0.35 mm off the back wall, either side of the cleat: lateral location +-0.35 mm.
- Plate thickness behind the pocket 3.15 mm (Y 35.05 to 38.2); lower plate Y 32.05 to 38.2 (6.15 mm), Z 30 to 51, 0.35 mm off the back wall. It is the lower bearing point that stops the node swinging back.
- Clearances (as modelled, centred): 0.35 mm normal to the bevel, 0.35 mm at the cleat's outer face, 0.35 mm at both cleat ends, 0.35 mm from the clip's inner face to the back wall. Measured minimum clip-to-sleeve gap 0.35 mm (model_measure_between).

How it carries the load: the cleat's underside bevel sits on the clip's bevel. The bevel pushes the node up and towards the glass, so the node cannot slide down (positive stop) and its top cannot pull away from the clip; the lower plate takes the back wall below it. As hung, the node settles 0.35-0.5 mm (rim top about 17.5-17.7 mm above the roof).
Fitting: hold the node with its back about 3 mm in front of the clip and its cleat just above the clip's pocket, move it back against the clip, and let it drop about 3 mm onto the bevel. Removing: lift the node about 3 mm (the cleat's lower edge must clear the lip tip, 2.2 mm above it as modelled), pull the back about 3 mm away, and lower it. The clip never passes the plinth (clip bottom Z 30, plinth top Z 9.5).

Support-free check:
- Sleeve, roof down: the cleat's top chamfer becomes a 45 deg overhang that grows out of the vertical back wall (at the 45 deg limit, self-supporting); the underside bevel faces up; the outer face and the ends are vertical. No bridge, nothing floating. Brow, key/eye holes, plinth inserts, roof ribs and the cap-side o-ring groove are untouched.
- Clip, hook top down: taper and chamfer faces are 45 deg, the pocket back wall and the cheeks are vertical, the block bottom faces up. The rim hook prints as before. **The one exception is a bridge:** the clip's lip tip (Z 54.155 as modelled) is an upward point, so upside down it is a low point: the first layer there spans 24.7 mm between the cheeks, and the bevel then grows towards the plate at 45 deg. PETG usually bridges 25 mm, but the tip may sag slightly. It is the edge of the bearing face, not the bearing face itself.
- Fallback if the bridge fails: split the cleat into two 10 mm wings with a 4.7 mm gap at the centre and add a 4 mm rib in the clip pocket from the lip up to the plate (two bridges of about 10 mm), or print the clip with a small support only under the lip tip.

Interference (assembly_inspect_interference over Sleeve, RimClip, FootCap, Sled, Hardware_Ref, Reference_Parts, Key_LightPipe, LeafFrame, SMA_Boot, USB_Bung, v19): no new hits. The 4 remaining overlaps are the expected ones from v16: inserts in their holes (Sleeve/Hardware_Ref 134 mm3, Sled/Hardware_Ref 38 mm3), nylon screw shanks in the standoffs (86 mm3), pigtail/bulkhead reference bodies (8 mm3). The old "clip touching the rail stop (0 volume)" contact is gone. The boards were not in this check (nested occurrences); the clip and cleat are outside the sleeve and do not reach them.

Not checked: real cleat strength (PETG, 24 x 3 mm bevel, node about 0.15-0.2 kg estimated; no FEA); the friction of the 45 deg faces as printed; whether the node rocks on the 0.35 mm clearances (a 0.5 mm TPU or foam pad on the lower plate would take the play out if it does).

## Foot-cap screws (v15)
Before: 2.0 mm deep counterbores, head 2.5 mm tall, so the heads stood 0.5 mm proud of the cap's underside. Now: counterbore 2.7 mm (5.5 mm dia), head seat at Z -1.8, head top at Z -4.3, so the heads sit 0.2 mm under the cap's flat underside (Z -4.5).
- Material left: under each head 1.3 mm (counterbore floor Z -1.8 to the sealing face Z -0.5; before 2.0 mm), round each counterbore: the 2.9 mm screw clearance hole is at the middle, the cap plate is the full 4.0 mm thick at the corners.
