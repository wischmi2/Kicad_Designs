## Notes
- **Thumbscrew (1):** M6 thread 30 mm long, 40 mm rod, spade head instead of the knurled head in the model. The coordinator checked by hand, not in Fusion: with 30 mm of thread, the nut stays engaged over the whole 6-19 mm wall range. The 10 mm unthreaded part sits under the head on the sump side. The spade head sits beyond the bridge on the sump side, so it has room. If you'd rather have a knurled head: B07QZBLMTR (black nylon knurled M6 x 30, 50 pcs, $9.99, slow delivery) or B0DSTBGVT3 (50 knurled with nuts and washers, $29.49, Prime).
- **M6 nut (2):** the captive pocket in the leg is 10.3 mm across the flats, made for a standard 10 mm M6 nut. The listing doesn't give the nut's width, so check it fits the pocket. The M6 x 25 bolts are spares.
- **M4 kit (3):** contains M4 x 6/8/10/12/15/20/25/30 (20 each), 80 nuts and 80 washers. The two M4 x 10 stop screws self-tap into the 3.4 mm holes in the leg. The Collet clamp uses M4 x 25 with a nut in the hex pocket.
- **Optional:** a 12 mm ID o-ring on the probe as a depth mark. Not on this list.
- **Wall pad:** printed in PETG for the draft (TPU later). Not bought.
