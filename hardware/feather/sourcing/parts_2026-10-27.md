4. AE1 2450AT18A100E (C89334): JLC marks it discontinued; 355 in stock, enough for 10 boards but not a long-term part. Johanson 2450AT18D0100E (C2917718, stock 2163) is 4-pin 3.2 x 1.6, so it is not a drop-in (footprint and keep-out change).
5. U6 nRF54L15-QFAA-R: C42458750, stock 1807, USD 3.40. Fine. Note the schematic MPN matches.
6. U4/U5 S2B-PH-K-S-GW are through-hole (JLC hand/wave step, may not be offered on all assembly tiers).
7. J3 MPN in the schematic ends in -TR; C448647 is the -TR part (stock 3136); C5155080 (no suffix, stock 1634, cheaper) is mechanically the same. Pick one and set the MPN to match. SW1 B3U-1000P: use C231329 (C271754 has stock 0).
8. C41 MPN "GRM155R60J106ME15" is missing the final D; JLC part is GRM155R60J106ME15D (C412251). C42/C41/C40 voltage ratings are 6.3 V/6.3 V/10 V: check against the VINT/LOAD/VBAT rails and DC-bias derating (not done here).
