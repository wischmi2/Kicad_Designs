nRF54L15 feather · resize proposals

Where the connectors go on a 50.8 × 22.86 mm board

Three ways to place all five current connectors on the standard Feather outline, with the four Feather mounting holes and no header rows. Every part is drawn to scale from its courtyard in today's PCB file. The ICs are shown only to give a sense of size; kicad-designer will place them.

Connector, top side Part on the bottom side Main ICs (size only) Antenna and keep-out Cable direction
Proposal A

Split long edges, closest to today

For

  • Solar and battery stay where they are today, on opposite long edges near the power ICs, so the AEM10330 loop stays short.
  • The sensor cable leaves on its own edge, away from the power cables.
  • Least re-routing of the three.

Against

  • The enclosure needs cable openings on both long sides plus the USB end.
  • The U4/U5 through-hole pins take a little room on the top side.
Proposal B

All cables out of one long edge

For

  • One cable side plus USB: the other long side of the enclosure can be closed or sit against a wall.
  • Neat if sens1 stacks above: battery, solar and sensor cables all fold up the same side.

Against

  • The three connectors fill the top edge from 7.6 to 32.6 mm, so plugs sit close together.
  • The power and sensor cables run side by side (CN1 carries the analog AIN signal).
Proposal C

Sensor out of the far end, antenna moved to a long edge

For

  • The sensor cable runs straight off the end, which suits sens1 sitting end to end with the main board.
  • USB and sensor cables at opposite ends.

Against

  • The antenna leaves the Feather's usual spot and sits on a long edge, with only about 3 mm of clearance to the board edge. The keep-out would need re-checking against the datasheet.
  • CN1 and its cable sit right next to the antenna end, so the cable can detune it. Riskiest of the three for radio range.
