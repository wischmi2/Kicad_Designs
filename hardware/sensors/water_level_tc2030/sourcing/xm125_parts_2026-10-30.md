XM125 datasheet v1.2 (read from the PDF): 28-pad LGA; VIN 1.8 V (1.71-1.89) OR 3.3 V (2.97-3.45) and VIN sets A121 IO and MCU level; separate 1V8 rail on pin 3 (1.71-1.89 V) always required; I/O range 0 to VIN+0.3 V; I2C_SCL pin 17, I2C_SDA 18, I2C_ADDRESS 20, NRESET 14 (active low), WAKE_UP 15; hibernate 17.7 uA, off 6.9 uA (1.8 V); VIN abs max 3.63 V.

Design note for kicad-designer: if VIN is fed from the 3.0 V VSENS rail it is only 30 mV above the 2.97 V minimum (VSENS tolerance must be checked). If that works, I/O is already at host level, no translators are needed, and only the 1.8 V LDO for pin 3 is required. If VIN is strapped to 1.8 V, the I2C and WAKE_UP/NRESET translators below are needed.
