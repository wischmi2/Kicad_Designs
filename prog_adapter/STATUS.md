## Purpose
One small board for the three programming jobs:
- **A. XM125 UART ONLY**: flash the XM125 on feather_water_level_tc2030 through its TC2030 pads with the FTDI 1.8 V cable (XM125_PROGRAMMING.md part 0).
- **B. SWD - Tag-Connect std**: TC2030-IDC cable (standard Tag-Connect SWD pinout) to a 10-pin 1.27 mm Cortex header, for the nRF9160 DK "Debug out" ribbon. For the planned TC2030 J3 on the next nRF54L15 feather revision.
- **C. 0.1 in SWD breakout**: same SWD nets on a 1x6 2.54 mm header for jumper wires to bare pads.
- **D. USB-C 3.0 V supply**: replaces the bench supply on TP9 VSENS + TP10 VBAT of the water level board.

## Pin maps
**J1 (section A), SSK05-BH-254-B3, wired 1:1 to the target TC2030 pins**
| J1 | Net | Goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | XM_NRST | SW1 to GND |
| 2 | XM_1V8 (target +1V8, reference only) | R3 1k -> JP1 -> J1.3 |
| 3 | XM_BOOT0 | JP1 |
| 4 | XM_RX (module input) | R1 330R <- FTDI ORG TXD |
| 5 | GND | FTDI BLK |
| 6 | XM_TX (module output) | R2 330R -> FTDI YEL RXD |

**J7 FTDI wire pads** (1.0 mm drill, 2.0 mm pads, 3.0 mm pitch, cable enters from the left board edge): 1 BLK GND, 2 YEL RXD, 3 ORG TXD. Two 1.5 x 3.2 mm slots ("ZIP") either side of the bundle for a 2.5 mm zip tie. **RED is left off: no anchor hole**, so there is nothing to solder it to by mistake; cut RED, BRN and GRN short and heat-shrink them (silk "RED/BRN/GRN: NC").

**J2 (section B), SSK05-BH-254-B3, Tag-Connect standard SWD** (TC2030-CTX datasheet: 1 VCC, 2 SWDIO, 3 nRESET, 4 SWCLK, 5 GND, 6 SWO):
1 SWD_VTREF, 2 SWD_SWDIO, 3 SWD_NRESET, 4 SWD_SWCLK, 5 GND, 6 SWD_SWO.

**J3 (section B), CNC Tech 3220-10-0100-00, ARM Cortex 10-pin**: 1 VTref, 2 SWDIO, 3 GND, 4 SWCLK, 5 GND, 6 SWO, 7 key/NC, 8 NC, 9 GND (GNDDetect tied to GND, as Tag-Connect does), 10 nRESET. Same mapping as Tag-Connect's TC2030-CTX cable.

**J4 (section C), 1x6 2.54 mm**: 1 VTref, 2 SWDIO, 3 SWCLK, 4 SWO, 5 nRESET, 6 GND (order picked for planar routing; each pin is labelled on silk). In parallel with J3/J2, stubs about 5 mm (J3-J4) and 15 mm (J4-J2): no concern at SWD clock rates.

**J5 (section D), 1x3 2.54 mm**: 1 3V0, 2 3V0, 3 GND. Silk "3V0 -> TP9+TP10", "GND -> TP8". Two 3V0 pins so TP9 and TP10 each get their own lead (guide part 5 ties both to the 3.0 V supply).

## Key / pin-1 checks
- **TC2030-IDC socket** (Tag-Connect TC2030-IDC rev B, sheet 2, end view): the polarising bump is in the middle of the long side next to the odd row (1-3-5); pin n of the socket = TC2030 pin n.
- **SSK05-BH-254-B3** (Same Sky datasheet rev 1.0, 2026-03-04, top view): 4.5 mm key notch in the long wall on the odd-row side, 1P at the end of that row, 2P across from it. The footprint (based on KiCad IDC-Header_2x03) has the notch on the odd column and the triangle at pin 1, same as the datasheet. So the cable bump only fits the notch with socket pin 1 on header pin 1. Checked by geometry: looking down on the header with the notch away from you, the socket face (seen from behind) puts pin 1 at the right end of the far row, and the datasheet top view rotated the same way also has 1P there.
- **J1 vs J2**: J1 is horizontal (rotated 270, notch toward the top edge) in the left half; J2 is vertical (notch toward J4) in the right half, about 33 mm apart. Different orientation and different silk titles.
- **J3 3220-10-0100-00** (CNC Tech drawing 3220-XX-0100-00 rev A): keying slot in the long wall on the odd-pin side (pins 1,3,5,7,9), triangle at pin 1; footprint has the slot on the odd column (outer board side) and a silk triangle at pin 1; drill 0.70 per the drawing. The Samtec FFSD ribbon has a polarizing key (DK listing); I did not get the FFSD drawing, so the key-to-pin-1 side of the ribbon is **not checked against its datasheet** (it follows the normal IDC convention, the same as above).

## nRF9160 DK Debug out (what the guide says)
Source: nRF9160 DK user guide v1.1.0 (docs.nordicsemi.com returned 403/404 to the tools; read from the web.archive.org copy of infocenter pages hw_debug_out.html and ext_programming_support.html, updated 2022-04-19).
- Debug out connector is **P19** (10-pin). "When the external board is powered up, the interface MCU will detect the supply voltage of the board and program/debug the target chip on the external board instead of the onboard nRF9160 and/or nRF52840."
- P19 pinout: 1 SWD0_SELECT ("Voltage supply from the external target, used as voltage detect input to the interface MCU"), 2 SWDIO, 3 GND, 4 SWCLK, 5 GND, 6 SWO, 7 N.C., 8 N.C., **9 N.C.**, 10 RESET.
- So the switch-over is **VTref sensing on pin 1 only**; there is no GND-detect (pin 9 is N.C. on the DK). The adapter passes the target VTref straight to J3 pin 1; nothing on the adapter loads it.
- "The voltage supported by external debugging/programming is the VDD voltage. This voltage can be selected to 1.8 V or 3 V using slide switch **SW9**." Make the DK VDD match the target: **SW9 = 3 V for the nRF54L15 feather (VDDIO 3.0 V)**. The external board must be powered by itself.
- If both P19 and P20 see target power, P19 wins.
- The DK can power the target through Debug out if **SB24** is shorted; leave SB24 open (not needed, and it would back-drive VTref).
- **No VTref/activity LED in section B**: there is no DK-side supply on the 10-pin connector (pin 1 is the target's voltage, used as an input by the DK), so any LED would load the target's VTref. Left out on purpose.
- **The 9160 DK's on-board J-Link (J-Link OB) only programs Nordic devices** (guide: "external boards with Nordic SoCs and SiPs"; SEGGER licence for OB probes). It cannot flash the XM125's STM32 over SWD; that is why section A uses the UART bootloader.
- Not confirmed: newer DK hardware revisions (the archived guide is v1.1.0); whether the DK ships a 10-pin ribbon (BOM lists the Samtec FFSD-05-D-06.00-01-N in case not).

## How to use
**A (XM125 UART)**: follow XM125_PROGRAMMING.md part 0. Solder the FTDI BLK/YEL/ORG wires into J7, zip-tie the bundle through the two slots. JP1 shunt on = BOOT0 pulled to the target +1V8 through 1k (bootloader); off = run. Power the water level board first (section D or bench), then plug the FTDI USB in. SW1 pulls NRST low (re-enter the bootloader without a power cycle while JP1 is on).
**B (SWD)**: TC2030-IDC plug on the target, its IDC socket on J2; 10-pin ribbon from DK P19 to J3; target powered; DK SW9 = target voltage. SW2 pulls nRESET low.
**C**: J4 jumper wires to bare SWD pads/test points (same nets as J2/J3; don't use J2 and J4 on two targets at once).
**D (3.0 V)**: USB-C power only (5.1k on CC1 and CC2, so a USB-C charger/port gives 5 V). F1 0.5 A PTC -> TLV75530 3.0 V (500 mA) -> J5. D1 lights when 3V0 is up (about 1 mA). Leads: J5.1 -> TP9 VSENS, J5.2 -> TP10 VBAT, J5.3 -> TP8 GND. FFC out first (guide part 5).
- **Separation decision**: the 3V0 net only touches U1 OUT, C2, R6 and J5. It is not connected to J1.2 (+1V8) or VTref. **GND is common** to all sections: the guide's setup already joins the board GND (TP8) with the FTDI GND, so a shared GND adds no new path. Do not plug a target into J1 that is powered from somewhere else at a different ground (none in the current setup).

## Decisions
- One GND for all sections (see above).
- J5 has two 3V0 pins (one lead each for TP9 and TP10) plus GND.
- RED FTDI wire: no anchor hole (left off).
- No LED in section B (no DK-side supply on Debug out).
- J3 is a through-hole shrouded keyed CNC Tech header (cheaper than the SMD Samtec FTSH-105-01-L-DV-K at $1.37 and fits the "through-hole where possible" rule).
- Board 72 x 48 mm instead of the 50 x 35 mm aim: four separate sections with large silk titles, pin legends next to every box header, finger room around the two IDC cables and buttons, and corner M3 holes. Same PCBWay price band (under 100 x 100 mm).
- kicad_dru: hole clearance 0.25 mm board-wide; 0.15 mm allowed only inside J6, where the KiCad/GCT USB4105 land pattern puts the 0.65 mm NPTH 0.175 mm from the GND pads (board-setup minimum lowered to 0.15 so this exception works).
